General Sexual Education

Comprehensive sexual education (CSE) is a curriculum-based process of teaching and learning about the cognitive, emotional, physical and social aspects of sexuality. It aims to equip children and young people with knowledge, skills, attitudes and values that will empower them to: realize their health, well-being and dignity; develop respectful social and sexual relationships; consider how their choices affect their own well-being and that of others; and, understand and ensure the protection of their rights throughout their lives.

  • Understanding your body and development
  • Consent and healthy relationships
  • Contraception and family planning
  • Personal safety and rights

Children's Sexuality Education

Sexuality education for children starts at home and in early childhood settings. It's about providing age-appropriate information that helps children understand their bodies, stay safe, and develop healthy attitudes toward themselves and others.

Early Childhood

Focus on correct names for body parts, understanding "good touch" and "bad touch", and recognizing different types of families.

Primary Years

Discussing puberty, emotional changes, and the importance of privacy and boundaries.

Key Prevention Tips

  • Use condoms consistently and correctly.
  • Get regular check-ups and screenings.
  • Communicate openly with partners about sexual health.
  • Consider vaccinations where available (e.g., HPV, Hepatitis B).
